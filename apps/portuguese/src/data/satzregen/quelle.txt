# Regi's Sentence Rain – the sentences, split into chunks by hand.
# scripts/build-satzregen.mjs turns this into src/data/satzregen/saetze.json.
#
# One line = one sentence:   chunk / chunk / chunk .  = note shown under the solved line  {flags}
#   Chunks are written as they stand inside the sentence ("eu", "ao sábado", "num café").
#   The first letter of slot 1 is capitalised in the display only. Portuguese has no opening ¿.
#   *  finite verb (or auxiliary)      ^  subject
#   ~  other verb part (infinitive after poder, querer …)
#   A pronoun after the verb is part of the verb chunk, with its hyphen (*visitam-me).
#   End: " ." or " ?" after the last chunk.
#   {alt 3 0 1 2}   another correct order, as chunk indices (several allowed)
#   {free 1<2 3-4}  every order of the chunks with these conditions, built into alts:
#                   1<2 = chunk 1 somewhere before chunk 2; 3-4 = chunk 3 right before chunk 4
#   {rule …}        own rule line for "These sentences were tricky"
#   {ptBR …}        note for a future Brazil mode (content-plan §1.5); not shown yet
#
# European Portuguese (pt-PT). The game must never reject a good sentence, but it only accepts
# what speakers in Portugal really say. What counts as correct:
#   statements  subject, verb, object; time or place may go to the front. A verb at the very end
#               is not accepted, and neither is an object before its verb.
#   questions   the question word comes first and the verb follows it straight away (Onde moras
#               tu?); the subject may also stand in front of the question word (Tu onde moras?).
#               "Onde tu moras?" is Brazilian and is not accepted.
#   yes/no      built like a statement (Tu falas inglês?); the verb may also come first.
#   pronouns    after the verb with a hyphen (ligo-te, deu-me); before the verb after não, já,
#               nunca, também, sempre, ainda and question words (não me deu, já te disse).
#   indirect    after onde, quando, como, se … the verb and the subject may come in either order
#               (não sei onde fica a estação / onde a estação fica).
# Modes keep their German ids: v2 = statements and wh-questions (A1), janein = yes/no questions
# (A1), klammer = where the object pronoun goes (A2), nebensatz = indirect questions (B1).
# Source: all sentences written for the game by the games run of the Portuguese course
# (2026-11-22), European Portuguese.
