# One line = one sentence:   chunk / chunk / chunk .  = note shown under the solved line  {flags}
#   Chunks are written as they stand inside the sentence ("I", "on Mondays", "your name").
#   The first letter of slot 1 is capitalised in the display only.
#   *  finite verb (or auxiliary/modal)      ^  subject
#   ~  other verb part (main verb after do/can/have …)
#   !  may not stand in slot 1
#   End: " ." or " ?" after the last chunk.
#   {alt 3 0 1 2}   another correct order, as chunk indices (several allowed)
#   {rule …}        own rule line for "These sentences were tricky"
# English has no verb-second "Vorfeld" rule, so the checker accepts only the canonical order
# and the {alt …} orders (src/lib/satzregen/check.js). Typical alt: the time phrase moved to
# the front ("On Mondays I play football."). Place before time is the canonical order.
# The note after " = " names the pattern of the sentence (subject, verb, object, place, time …).
# Modes keep their German ids: v2 = statements and wh-questions (A1), janein = yes/no
# questions (A1), klammer = auxiliaries and modal verbs (A2), nebensatz = indirect questions (B1).
